What this is

Every set includes an edit log that states what changed, what did not change, and whether disclosure is required. For this example set:
  • All five images: Exempt (lighting, color, straighten, limited personal-item reduction)
  • No structural changes, no added or removed furniture, no view modifications
  • Originals retained and referenced

The goal is photos that are useful for a listing and defensible if the MLS or a buyer asks what was done.
