Compliance record

Every set comes with an edit log that states what changed, what did not change, and whether disclosure is required. For this example set:

  • All five images: Exempt (lighting, color, straighten, limited personal-item reduction)
  • No structural changes, no added or removed furniture, no view modifications
  • Originals retained and referenced
